## The Quick Answer

**Perplexity** if your primary use case is research, fact-finding, or "what does X actually say about Y?" Every claim gets a source link, and the source-following is genuinely good.

**ChatGPT** if you want a more general assistant. Image generation, voice mode, custom GPTs, Deep Research — broader capabilities, weaker default citation rigor.

Many researchers subscribe to both — Perplexity for finding sources, ChatGPT for synthesizing them.

## Pricing

**Edge: tie.**

## Cited answers

This is Perplexity's core competence and where it wins decisively:
- Every claim in an answer gets a numbered inline citation
- Citations link to the source URL — you can verify them
- "Sources" panel shows all referenced documents
- Hallucination rates are demonstrably lower than ChatGPT for factual queries

ChatGPT can cite sources when browsing is enabled, but the rigor is weaker — sometimes it invents references or paraphrases without attribution.

**Edge: Perplexity** decisively for any work where source quality matters.

## Conversational reasoning

ChatGPT wins. It's the more capable general reasoner:
- Multi-step problem solving
- Complex math and logic
- Code generation and debugging
- Creative writing

Perplexity's models (Sonar, sometimes Claude/GPT under the hood) are competent but tuned for retrieval-augmented Q&A, not deep reasoning.

**Edge: ChatGPT.**

## Image and voice

ChatGPT has:
- DALL-E image generation built-in
- Conversational voice mode with interruption
- Image understanding via vision

Perplexity has:
- Some image generation (FLUX, Stable Diffusion)
- No voice mode
- Image upload + analysis

For multimodal workflows, ChatGPT is meaningfully more developed.

**Edge: ChatGPT.**

## Custom GPTs / Spaces

ChatGPT has Custom GPTs — over a million pre-built assistants with knowledge files and actions. Build your own with one click.

Perplexity has Spaces — collections of sources you can chat with. Useful for ongoing research projects but less extensible.

**Edge: ChatGPT** for ecosystem; **Perplexity** for research-focused workflows.

## Deep Research mode

Both ship "deep research" modes that produce long structured reports:

**Perplexity Deep Research**: Pulls from 30+ sources, generates 8-15 page reports, every claim cited. Genuinely impressive for academic-style research.

**ChatGPT Deep Research**: Comparable depth, broader source variety (including paywalled content via plugins), slightly weaker citation discipline.

For academic / journalistic research where citation rigor matters, Perplexity edges ahead. For broad business research, ChatGPT covers more ground.

**Edge: Perplexity** for rigor; **ChatGPT** for breadth.

## Speed

Perplexity is fast. Most queries return a cited answer in 5-15 seconds. ChatGPT with browsing enabled often takes 30-60 seconds for similar queries.

For "look something up and tell me the answer with sources" workflows, the speed gap is noticeable.

**Edge: Perplexity.**

## Mobile app

Both have polished mobile apps. Perplexity's app is more focused — search bar, results, follow-up. ChatGPT's app is a full assistant with voice, image gen, and chat history sync.

For research on mobile, Perplexity is faster and cleaner. For general AI on mobile, ChatGPT is more capable.

**Edge: Perplexity** for research; **ChatGPT** for general use.

## Privacy and data

Both have paid-tier policies that don't train on your data by default. Both offer enterprise modes with zero retention.

Perplexity is smaller and less scrutinized. ChatGPT has the more mature enterprise compliance posture (SOC 2, GDPR, etc.).

**Edge: ChatGPT** for enterprise.

## Bottom line

**Use Perplexity if:**
- You research for a living (journalism, academia, due diligence)
- You need citations you can verify
- You want fast factual Q&A with sources
- Your primary AI use is "looking things up"

**Use ChatGPT if:**
- You want a versatile general-purpose assistant
- Image generation, voice, custom GPTs matter
- You do creative writing or coding
- You want one tool that does most things

**Use both** if you can afford $40/month. Perplexity for finding facts, ChatGPT for everything else. They're complementary in a research workflow.
