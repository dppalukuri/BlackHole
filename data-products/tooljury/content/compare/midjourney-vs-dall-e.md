## The Quick Answer

**Midjourney** if you want the best aesthetic quality and you're producing art, illustration, or design work. Industry standard for professional creative use.

**DALL-E** (via ChatGPT) if you want one tool that handles text + images conversationally, follows prompts more literally, and integrates with your AI assistant workflow. Better for utility.

Many serious creatives use both for different stages of work.

## Pricing

| Plan | Midjourney | DALL-E (via ChatGPT) |
|------|-----------|----------------------|
| Free | None | Limited via ChatGPT free tier |
| Entry | Basic $10/month (~200 images) | ChatGPT Plus $20/month (unlimited DALL-E) |
| Pro | $30/month (Standard, 15 hrs fast) | Same $20/month |
| Mega / Team | $60/month (30 hrs fast) | Team $25/user/month |
| API access | Via partners only | OpenAI API, ~$0.04-$0.12/image |

DALL-E is meaningfully cheaper if you're already paying for ChatGPT Plus — there's no incremental cost. Midjourney is a standalone subscription.

**Edge: DALL-E** on price if you have ChatGPT Plus; **Midjourney** has more generation hours at the Standard tier for image-heavy work.

## Aesthetic quality

Midjourney's defaults look like a professional photographer or illustrator made them. DALL-E's defaults look like a competent AI generator. This gap is the single biggest reason Midjourney remains dominant in creative industries.

In blind A/B tests with designers, art directors, and illustrators, Midjourney wins ~70% of preference for "use this in real client work" prompts. DALL-E wins ~30% — mostly for utility/literal scenes.

**Edge: Midjourney** decisively.

## Prompt following

Inverse picture. DALL-E follows instructions more literally:
- Specific quantities ("three people, two dogs") — DALL-E gets it right more often
- Spatial relationships ("X to the left of Y") — DALL-E is more reliable
- Text in images ("a sign that says HELLO") — DALL-E renders text far better
- Specific objects ("a 1967 Mustang") — DALL-E more accurate

Midjourney sometimes "creatively interprets" your prompt, which is great for art and frustrating for utility.

**Edge: DALL-E.**

## Editing existing images

**DALL-E** has integrated editing through ChatGPT conversation:
- "Make the sky darker" / "Add a person on the left"
- Inpainting via masking
- Iterative refinement in one chat

**Midjourney** has variation, vary region, vary subtle/strong, and zoom out — powerful but requires more steps.

For conversational refinement, DALL-E feels more natural. For granular pro editing, Midjourney's region tools are more precise.

**Edge: DALL-E** for casual editing; **Midjourney** for fine control.

## Style consistency

**Midjourney** has `--sref` (style reference) — you can reference a previous image to lock its aesthetic across new generations. Critical for series work, character sheets, brand consistency.

**DALL-E** has improved consistency but still drifts more across generations.

**Edge: Midjourney** clearly for any series or brand work.

## Resolution

Both currently output at ~1024x1024 native, with upscaling options:
- **Midjourney:** Up to 2048×2048 native (v7), or upscale via Magnific/Topaz
- **DALL-E:** 1024x1024, 1024x1792, 1792x1024 native

For poster/print quality work, both need external upscaling. Neither is print-ready at native resolution.

**Edge: tie** with caveats; both need upscaling for large prints.

## Workflow integration

**DALL-E** lives inside ChatGPT — generation happens in conversation alongside text:
- "Write me a blog post and generate a header image"
- "Iterate on this design 5 more times"
- Auto-incorporates into your existing chat history

**Midjourney** has Discord (legacy) and a web alpha. Discord is the more polished interface; the web alpha is improving.

If you want AI image gen as part of your AI assistant workflow, DALL-E wins. If you want a dedicated image gen tool, Midjourney's web is competitive.

**Edge: DALL-E** for integrated workflows.

## NSFW / content policy

Both have content policies. Midjourney is moderately strict (no nudity, no copyrighted characters by name). DALL-E is stricter (no public figures, no copyrighted characters at all).

For commercial work involving any borderline content, you'll hit fewer walls with Midjourney. For SFW everything, both are fine.

**Edge: Midjourney** for commercial flexibility.

## Speed

DALL-E: ~10-15 seconds per image.
Midjourney v7: ~30-60 seconds per image in Fast mode.

DALL-E is meaningfully faster. For iterative work where you need to generate dozens of variations quickly, this matters.

**Edge: DALL-E.**

## Bottom line

**Use Midjourney if:**
- You're a designer, illustrator, or creative professional
- Aesthetic quality is the primary concern
- You need style consistency across a series
- You're already paying for it (which you should be for serious art work)

**Use DALL-E if:**
- You want utility image generation alongside ChatGPT
- Prompt accuracy matters more than aesthetics
- Conversational editing is appealing
- You already pay for ChatGPT Plus — DALL-E is essentially free

**Use both** for serious creative work — DALL-E for ideation, Midjourney for the polished output.
